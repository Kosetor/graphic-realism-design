# Agent scenario: Graphics

## Goal

Постеры, обложки, thumbnails, social cards, brand frames — original Graphic Realism graphics.

## Composition pillars

1. **Silhouette first** — large geometric mark or wordmark
2. **One accent** — signal OR volt OR cyan
3. **Industrial chrome** — corners, hazard, brackets
4. **Negative space** — do not fill 100%
5. **Readable at thumb size**

## Formats

| Format | Size |
|--------|------|
| Poster | 1080×1350 / A3 SVG |
| Thumb | 1280×720 |
| Square | 1080×1080 |
| Story | 1080×1920 |
| OG card | 1200×630 |

## Layers (bottom → top)

1. Void or white field
2. Background pattern ≤8% (`backgrounds/`)
3. Primary shape / figure / wordmark
4. Accent bar or hazard strip
5. Corner registration
6. CAPS label + mono ID
7. Optional CTA chip

## Wordmark rules

- Display font, tight leading
- Tracking slight on CAPS
- Avoid long sentences on poster
- Subline mono small

## Do / Don’t

**Do:** hard geometry, sport energy, limited palette, SVG sharpness  
**Don’t:** stock cyberpunk neon city, lens flares, fake Bungie marks, heavy photo filters
